Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to enter my password?

Aura does not perform login attempts, authentication requests, or account access. Passwords are analyzed only for strength characteristics such as length, entropy, and patterns.

Why do you ask where the password is used?

Risk depends on context. A weak password on email or financial accounts carries higher impact than the same password elsewhere.

Should I enter passwords I don’t own?

No. Only submit passwords you own or are authorized to test.
