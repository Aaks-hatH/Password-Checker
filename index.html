Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you require where the password is used?

Password risk depends on context. A weak password on a forum has different consequences than the same password used for email, banking, or school systems. This allows Aura to assess severity accurately without accessing any account.

Is it safe to enter my password here?

Aura does not perform login attempts, authentication requests, or account validation.

Passwords are analyzed for strength characteristics only (length, entropy, patterns, and resistance estimates). Aura does not attempt to access external services.

Should I enter passwords I don’t own?

No. You should only submit passwords you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Submitting third-party credentials is prohibited.

Does Aura guarantee my account is secure?

No. Aura provides informational analysis only. Real-world security depends on many factors including MFA, platform defenses, and user behavior.
